Causal Tongue-Tie

LLMs Can Encode Causal Direction, But Their Yes/No Outputs Fail to Express

Ziyi Ding1, Xiao-Ping Zhang1,*

* Corresponding author.

Wrong Yes/No, recoverable causal direction. Across 8 instruction-tuned LLMs (0.5B–72B), frozen hidden-state probes reach ≥96.9% on anti-commonsense CLADDER while spoken Yes/No stalls at 35.0%–52.5%.

2×2 Diagnostic

Separate internal recoverability from external expression.

01 · Cell (b)

Fit on CS, Freeze Everywhere

Fit logistic probe $g_w(h)$ and subspace $V_{\mathrm{cs}}$ on commonsense items once; test frozen on anti-CS with zero LLM updates.

$$\Delta = \mathrm{Acc}_{\mathrm{probe}}(\ell^\star) - \mathrm{Acc}_{\mathrm{out}} \approx +0.50$$
02 · Mediation

Used Internally, Blocked Out

Erasing $V_{\mathrm{cs}}$ collapses paired counterfactual KL by $-96.3\%$; full layer-27 donor patching flips $57.1\%$ of wrong answers.

$$h_i^{(\ell)} \leftarrow \big(I - V_{\mathrm{cs}}V_{\mathrm{cs}}^\top\big)\,h_i^{(\ell)}$$
03 · Interface

Contrast Directed Edges

Reframing Yes/No into A/B propositions still fails ($37.5\%$); contrasting $c \to e$ vs. $e \to c$ edges unlocks $98.8\%$.

$$\mathrm{Acc}_{\mathrm{YN}}\,(26.3\%) \;\ll\; \mathrm{Acc}_{c\to e \text{ vs. } e\to c}\,(98.8\%)$$

Scope

Diagnostic measurement, not a universal repair

Operational definition: Linear recoverability diagnoses a representation–output gap on controlled textual queries, not human-like understanding or raw sensor causal discovery.

Control & templates: Scalar $V_{\mathrm{cs}}$ swaps do not flip Yes/No alone (full L27 patching flips 57.1%); linear probe directions remain template- and layer-conditioned (cross-template mean 0.672).
