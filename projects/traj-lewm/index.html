Traj-LeWM

Path-Aware World-Model Planning via Latent Trajectory Cost

Endpoint distance ignores how a trajectory reaches its goal. Traj-LeWM adds a lightweight (<1M-parameter) Latent Trajectory Cost that shapes the visual encoder and re-ranks CEM candidate paths.

Method

Path-level supervision & ranking in <1M parameters.

01 · Path Functional

Goal-Conditioned LTC

Aggregates state $z_t$, step change $\Delta z_t$, goal residual $d_t^g = z_g - z_t$, and phase $\eta_t$ across the rollout.

$$S_\psi(\tau \mid g) = \frac{1}{T}\sum_{t=0}^{T-1}\ell_\psi(z_t, \Delta z_t, d_t^g, \eta_t)$$
02 · Representation

Preference Supervision

Contrasts expert paths against goal mismatches, fixed-endpoint jitter, and mined closed-loop CEM failures.

$$\mathcal{L} = \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{LeWM}} + \lambda_{\mathrm{path}}\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{path}} + \lambda_{\mathrm{mined}}\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{mined}}$$
03 · Planning

IQR-Calibrated Ranking

Aligns LTC scale with endpoint distance $C_0$ across the CEM candidate pool at test time.

$$C_\lambda(\hat{\tau}, g) = C_0(\hat{\tau}, g) + \lambda\frac{\mathrm{IQR}(C_0)}{\mathrm{IQR}(S_\psi)}\,S_\psi(\hat{\tau} \mid g)$$

Scope

Complementary signal & boundaries

Lightweight regime: LTC supplements endpoint distance without pretrained visual backbones; frozen DINOv2 features still lead on Two-Room (100% vs. 94%).

Real-robot sample: The 20 paired Franka FR3 trials (14/20 vs. 10/20) provide preliminary physical feasibility evidence rather than a large-sample test.
