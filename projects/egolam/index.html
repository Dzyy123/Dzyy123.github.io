Three-Part Design

Preserve wrist manipulation while factoring out observer head motion.

01 · Hand Queries

Slot Pooling & 3D Wrist Readout

Left- and right-hand queries pool 8 frozen DINOv2 object slots across $(o_t, o_{t+\Delta})$; a bottleneck head supervises 3D wrist translation and rotation $a_t$ in the earlier head frame.

$$z_t = f_\theta(q_{t+\Delta} - q_t), \qquad \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{action}} = \operatorname{MSE}_{\mathrm{valid}}\!\big(g_{\mathrm{hand}}(z_t),\, a_t\big)$$
02 · Disentanglement

Head-Conditioned FDM & GRL

Measured camera motion $n_t$ explains apparent shifts in the forward model, while gradient reversal and synthetic 2D warps ($\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{inv}}$) suppress camera leakage in $z_t$.

$$\hat{S}_{t+\Delta} = \operatorname{FDM}(S_t, z_t, n_t), \qquad \hat{n}_t = g_{\mathrm{cam}}\!\big(\operatorname{GRL}(z_t)\big)$$
03 · Imitation

RGB-Only Extraction & Policy

Inference extracts $z_t$ from two RGB frames without pose labels. Policies pretrain on frozen teacher codes $\tilde{z}_t$ before joint 7-DoF control fine-tuning.

$$\hat{u}_t = D_\psi\!\big(\pi_\phi(o_{\le t})\big), \qquad \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{ctrl}} = \ell_a(\hat{u}_t, u_t)$$

Scope & Boundaries

Supervision & real-turn diagnostics

Train-only pose labels, RGB-only deployment: Stage 2 uses EgoVerse wrist/camera poses and hand keypoints where available ($1{,}056.9\,\mathrm{h}$ LAM pool), while inference and policy labeling require only two RGB frames $(o_t, o_{t+\Delta})$.

Real head turns & batching: On matched EgoVerse clips with large head turns ($\ge 15^\circ$), EgoLAM curbs wrist translation error growth relative to LAPA ($+2.75$ vs. $+6.80\,\mathrm{cm}$ held-out), whereas rotation contrasts span zero; single-pair cached latency leads ($0.85$ vs. $1.47\,\mathrm{ms}$), while LAOM is faster at batch size $32$ ($8.42$ vs. $10.50\,\mathrm{ms}$).
