Decoupled Formulation

Separate the recurrent rollout carrier from planner readouts.

01 · Coupling Diagnosis

Latent Readout Feedback

Autoregressive predictors feed $\hat{z}_{t+k} = z_{t+k}^{\mathrm{tgt}} + e_k$ back into $P_\phi$, propagating readout error via local Jacobians $J_k$.

$$e_{k+1} \approx J_k e_k + \epsilon_k, \quad J_k = \left.\frac{\partial P_\phi(z, a_{t+k})}{\partial z}\right|_{z = z_{t+k}^{\mathrm{tgt}}}$$
02 · Rollout Carrier

Persistent Hidden Trajectory

Initialized via $h_{\mathrm{init}} = g_\phi(z_t)$, a GRU advances $h_{0:H-1}$ over action embeddings $u_k$ while $R_\phi$ projects $\hat{z}_{t+k+1}$ one-way.

$$h_k = F_\phi(h_{k-1}, u_k), \qquad \hat{z}_{t+k+1} = R_\phi(h_k)$$
03 · Supervision

Horizon-Aligned Objective

Every readout along $h_{0:H-1}$ is supervised jointly across the planning horizon while retaining backbone losses $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{aux}}$.

$$\mathcal{L} = \frac{1}{H}\sum_{k=1}^{H} \operatorname{MSE}\!\big(\hat{z}_{t+k},\, z_{t+k}^{\mathrm{tgt}}\big) + \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{aux}}$$

Scope

Short-range contact vs. horizon stability

Contact-rich trade-off: LeWM-PushT (94.67% $\to$ 87.33%) is the sole setting where SRD trails AR, reflecting PushT’s sensitivity to fine short-range contact pose; on PLDM-PushT, SRD gains +6.67 pp (84.67%) and SRD-AR reaches 89.33% (+11.33 pp).

Optional local supervision: SRD-AR adds an auxiliary one-step loss $\lambda_{\mathrm{AR}}\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{AR}}$ during training while keeping test-time rollout decoupled, peaking at 99.33% on PLDM-TwoRoom at extra training parameter cost.
