GeoRefiner

GeoRefiner: Geometry-Conditioned Plug-and-Play Action Refinement for Vision-Language-Action Models

Pretrained VLAs capture task intent yet miss local 3D contact geometry under perturbations. GeoRefiner attaches downstream of any frozen base VLA to apply gated, geometry-conditioned residual corrections.

Architecture & Staged Learning

Decouple 3D geometric precision from the frozen VLA backbone.

01 · Canonicalization

0.9M Benchmark Adapter

Fixed converters and 4-layer MLPs align heterogeneous robot states, global/wrist RGB, and actions into a canonical 7-DoF frame.

$$a_t = [\Delta x,\, \Delta y,\, \Delta z,\, \Delta r_x,\, \Delta r_y,\, \Delta r_z,\, g]$$
02 · Refinement

Gated Bounded Residual

Trajectory tokens $T$ cross-attend to DINOv2 geometry $G$ and QFormer region tokens $R$ to predict gate $\gamma$ and bounded $\Delta A$.

$$A_{\mathrm{refined}} = A_{\mathrm{nominal}} + \gamma \odot \Delta A, \quad \Delta A = s \cdot \tanh\!\big(\operatorname{MLP}_{\mathrm{res}}(\operatorname{LN}(H))\big)$$
03 · Progressive Stages

Three-Stage Training

Stage 1 pretrains depth and Grounded-SAM region heads; Stage 2 learns gated residuals; Stage 3 tunes only 0.9M adapter weights.

$$\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{stage2}} = \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{action}} + \lambda_{\mathrm{gate}}\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{gate}} + \lambda_{\mathrm{smooth}}\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{smooth}}$$

Scope

Local trajectory refinement vs. high-level planning

Intent assumption: GeoRefiner corrects local positional, orientational, and contact deviations assuming the base VLA’s nominal trajectory reflects valid task intent; high-level semantic or subgoal planning errors remain outside its scope.

Gate & region necessity: Removing the gate $\gamma$ causes over-correction under lighting shifts (−2.3 pp on LIBERO-Plus Light), while dropping QFormer region tokens $R$ cuts OpenVLA-OFT gain from +10.1 to +3.2 pp.
