Three-Stage Workflow

Align intervention units before orienting edges and fitting mechanisms.

01 · Calibration

Dimension-Wise Invertible Map $T$

Keep $E_\theta$ and $D_\psi$ frozen; fit a strictly monotone map $T_{\phi,j}$ from round-robin verified targets ($B_{\mathrm{cal}}$) so visual readouts $h(x)$ match command units $q_j(u)$.

$$h(x) = \frac{T(E_\theta(x)) - \mu^c}{\sigma^c}, \qquad g_j > \sum_{l=1}^{L}|w_{jl}|$$
02 · Discovery

Active CPDAG Orientation

Freeze $T$, estimate an observational CPDAG on $h(\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{obs}})$, and target incident unoriented edges ($B_{\mathrm{graph}}$) to resolve direction before Meek propagation.

$$e_{j \to k} = \big|\operatorname{Corr}(h_j, h_k;\, \mathcal{I}_j)\big|, \qquad B = B_{\mathrm{cal}} + B_{\mathrm{graph}}$$
03 · Counterfactuals

Residual-Fixed $T^{-1}$ Decoding

Hold factual residuals $\eta_j^{\mathrm{fact}}$ fixed, propagate $q_j(u)$ to descendants along $\hat{G}$, and invert calibration via $T^{-1}$ back to decoder coordinates.

$$\hat{x}^{\mathrm{cf}} = D_\psi\!\Big(T^{-1}\!\big(\sigma^c \odot h^{\mathrm{cf}} + \mu^c\big)\Big)$$

Scope & Controls

Assumptions & conditional evaluation

Known node semantics & executed queries: Concept labels anchor node identities during encoder pretraining without revealing $G^\star$. Tables 3–4 score conditional errors on executable queries rather than pooled end-to-end rates, while Table 2 isolates calibration on a matched 3-seed Domino cohort sharing $E_\theta$, $\hat{G}$, and $D'$.

Readout error vs. causal effect: Better state-readout MSE alone does not guarantee better intervention predictions—ordinary joint updating lowers readout MSE ($11.36$ vs. $12.86$) while degrading amplitude-shift counterfactual effect NMSE ($20.45$ vs. $6.52$).
