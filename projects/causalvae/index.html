Three-Stage Design

Causal structure without redesigning the host model.

01 · Structure

Frozen-Host DAG Layer

Freeze the pretrained encoder and transition; organize latent variables through a differentiable acyclic graph $A$.

$$\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{DAG}}(A) = \operatorname{tr}\!\big(\exp(A \odot A)\big) - d$$
02 · Identifiability

Train-Only State Anchor

Simulator state $s_t$ anchors coordinate axes during training ($\hat{A} = A^\star$ under Thm. 1); inference needs no state labels.

$$\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{align}} = \|g_{\mathrm{align}}(\tilde{z}_t) - s_t\|_2^2$$
03 · Rollout

$\alpha$-Gated Transition Fusion

Blend host latents $z_t$ with causal latents $\tilde{z}_t$ during Stage-3 refinement to stabilize 5- and 10-step rollouts.

$$z_t^{\mathrm{gate}} = (1 - \alpha_t)\,z_t + \alpha_t\,\tilde{z}_t$$

Scope

Selective gains & boundaries

Backbone-dependent: Gains peak on intervention-fragile hosts (Physics GNN/AE-NLL, Shapes GNN-NLL). Narrow host latents can discard factors on Chemistry Modular-C; widening latent capacity reverses that drop.

Local linear trend: Theorem 1 holds at the population optimum under (I1–I5), and $A_{\mathrm{GT}} = |J(s^\star) - I|$ reflects local first-order coupling rather than full nonlinear laws.
